Linux that grows up with your kid.

A configuration layer on top of Omarchy — not a kids' tablet, a real system.

GitHub Author DE

Early development · not usable yet

omarchy-kids layers configuration on top of Omarchy — age-tiered desktop profiles that grow with your kid, a parent-side control center, and sane screen time. Not a fork, not a walled-garden kids' app: Omarchy installs normally, omarchy-kids adds config, a background agent, and a control center on top.

Age tiers

Desktop profiles that grow with the child — from first click to their own machine.

Parental control

A control center on the parent's machine, talking to the child's agent over SSH.

Screen time

Sane limits instead of blunt locks — on a real, free Linux system.

omarchy-kids is an independent project built on Omarchy — not officially affiliated with Omarchy or 37signals.
